A selective record of documented exchange, not a complete census. Connection view is qualitative: blocks move without resizing, routes are not drawn, and region-to-region distance is illustrative rather than measured.

Connection was never evenly shared. Trade and ideas moved alongside conquest, colonial rule, forced migration and disease. Some ties expanded while others narrowed. The connection view shows qualitative shifts in documented regional ties; it is not a measure of trade volume or the experience of every country in a region.
