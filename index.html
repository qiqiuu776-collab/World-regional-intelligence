A selective record, not a complete census. Between dated moments, the map shows the previous entry; lines show broad corridors, not exact paths or trade volume.

Connection was never evenly shared. Trade and ideas moved alongside conquest, colonial rule, forced migration and disease. Some routes expanded while others narrowed. The map follows documented episodes rather than treating history as a steady march toward one connected world.
