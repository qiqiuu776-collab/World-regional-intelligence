Briefing structure

  1. What happened? Summarise the event neutrally and include the date.
  2. What is confirmed? Separate verified information from claims and forecasts.
  3. Why does it matter? Explain historical, political or economic context.
  4. Who are the stakeholders? Identify governments, institutions, companies and affected groups.
  5. What could happen next? Present plausible scenarios and uncertainties.
  6. Sources Link primary documents and corroborating reporting.
