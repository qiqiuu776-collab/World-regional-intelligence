Sources & Research Standards

A transparent sourcing framework helps readers evaluate the reliability, date and limits of each claim.

Preferred source hierarchy

  1. Primary sources: laws, government statements, official statistics and international agreements.
  2. International organisations: UN agencies, World Bank, IMF, OECD and regional institutions.
  3. Academic research: peer-reviewed journals, university research centres and scholarly books.
  4. Specialist institutes: established foreign-policy, security and economic research organisations.
  5. News reporting: reputable outlets for timely developments, ideally corroborated independently.

Minimum citation practice

  • Provide publisher, title, publication date and direct URL.
  • Use the original document where possible.
  • For statistics, record unit, time period and release date.
  • Label uncertainty, disputed claims and analysis clearly.
  • Do not cite AI-generated summaries as evidence.
