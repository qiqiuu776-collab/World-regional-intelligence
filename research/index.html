Suggested policy brief structure

  1. Executive summary: the question and main finding.
  2. Issue background: what happened and why now.
  3. Stakeholder map: interests, influence and constraints.
  4. Policy options: realistic choices and trade-offs.
  5. Implications: political, economic, diplomatic and business effects.
  6. Assessment: evidence-based judgement, uncertainty and scenarios.
  7. References: traceable sources and publication dates.
Label your own judgement explicitly and distinguish it from source-reported facts.
