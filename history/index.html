Research structure

  • Chronology: date, event and key actors.
  • Context: domestic and international conditions.
  • Consequences: immediate outcomes and long-term legacies.
  • Debates: competing interpretations and contested memories.
  • Sources: academic works, archives and primary documents.

Avoid implying that present outcomes were inevitable. Distinguish contemporary evidence from later interpretation.
